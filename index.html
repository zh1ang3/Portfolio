Panty&Stocking Title Sequence

Timeline
October 22 – December 16
Skills
Graphic Design, Animation, Storytelling
Tools
After Effects, Illustrator

Overview

For this project, I created a title sequence inspired by Panty & Stocking with Garterbelt. My goal was to reinterpret the visual identity of the series through a fast-paced motion piece that captures its bold, chaotic, and playful personality. I focused on combining graphic design, typography, color, and animation to create something that feels energetic while still being visually organized.

The Problem

The challenge was to create a title sequence that captured the bold and energetic style of Panty & Stocking while working primarily with vector art. Since the original series has a very distinct anime style with exaggerated characters, bright colors, and detailed visuals, I needed to simplify these elements without losing the personality of the source material.

The Solution

I recreated key visual elements as vector art and used simplified shapes, bold outlines, and bright colors to build a consistent visual style throughout the sequence. I then animated these vector elements through movement, scaling, rotation, and transitions to create a fast-paced title sequence. Using vector art also allowed me to easily adjust the size, position, and the individual elements of each composition while still keeping everything clean and consistent.

What I’ve Learned

The biggest thing I learned from this project was how much I could simplify and still have it feel like Panty & Stocking. The show has so much detail, so I had to figure out which parts actually matter, like the colors, the outlines, and the overall shapes of things. Working in vector also made animating way easier, because I could move, scale, and re-time pieces in After Effects without having to redraw anything. It showed me that a lot of the energy in a title sequence comes from the timing and movement.
